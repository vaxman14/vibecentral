Cookie policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · applies to the website vibecentral.app

No cookies

This website does not set cookies, first-party or third-party. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

What is stored in your browser instead

  • Theme preference. When you switch between light and dark with the button in the header, the choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key vc-theme. It stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and you can clear it by clearing site data in your browser.

Third parties

  • Google Fonts serves the Inter typeface. Font requests do not set cookies.
  • Netlify hosts the site and processes the request form. Netlify does not set cookies on plain static sites like this one.
  • GitHub and Buy Me a Coffee are linked from the page. Cookies they set apply only once you are on their sites, under their own policies.

The dashboard is different

A vibecentral dashboard that you install yourself sets one session cookie to keep you signed in to your own copy, on your own domain. That is described in that copy's own privacy page, served at /privacy on the domain where it runs, and has nothing to do with this website.
